# Executive Summary

Members earn points on branch purchases and can see their balance and history online. Points earned on a purchase are taken back after a refund of that purchase that the Refunds Portal reports as paid; a refund made outside the Refunds Portal takes no points back in this release. [UC-02 BR-1, BR-3, and BR-4](./06a-use-cases-member.md#uc-02-view-points-history) state when, and how many.

## Business Objectives

1. Members trust their points balance. Measured by NFR-01: zero balance complaints upheld per month.
2. Members can check their points at any time without calling a branch. Its measure and starting figure are open (13 / OI-12).
