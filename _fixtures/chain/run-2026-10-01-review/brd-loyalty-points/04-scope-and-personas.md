# Project Scope

Members see their points balance and the history of their points movements online.

## In Scope

- Earning points on branch purchases.
- Points balance and history for members.
- Taking points back after a refund of a purchase that the Refunds Portal reports as paid.

## Out of Scope

- Redeeming points (a later phase; 12 Wishlist). Until then the balance screen tells members that points cannot be spent yet (11).
- Joining the loyalty program and member sign-in. Members use their existing loyalty program account.
- Taking points back after a refund made outside the Refunds Portal: at a branch till, in cash, after the refund window, for a purchase not paid by card, or by voiding a sale.

---

# Personas / Actors

| Persona | Role | Key Goals | Access Level |
|---------|------|-----------|-------------|
| Member | Customer in the loyalty program | Know their points balance and where it comes from | Own points only |
