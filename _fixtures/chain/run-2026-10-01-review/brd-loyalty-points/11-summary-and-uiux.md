# Summary

Members see their points balance and every points movement, including points taken back after a refund.

# UI/UX Expectations

- Points are whole numbers; negative movements show a minus sign.
- The screens work on phones and computers.
- While redeeming points is out of scope (04), the balance screen says that points cannot be spent yet.
