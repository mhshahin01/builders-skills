# Executive Summary

Customers of our retail branches ask for refunds in person today. Staff record requests on paper, managers approve them by phone, and customers wait up to 10 days without knowing where their request stands. The Refunds Portal lets customers request and track refunds online, and lets branch managers approve or reject them in one place. Approved refunds are paid back to the customer's original card through our payment provider.

# Background and Context

About 1,200 refund requests a month reach our 40 branches. 18% of complaints to customer care are about slow or lost refund requests.

# Business Objectives

1. Cut the average time from request to payout from 10 days to 3 days.
2. Stop lost refund requests: every request made in the portal is recorded and visible to the customer. Refunds handled at a branch outside the portal stay outside this objective in this release (04 Out of Scope).
3. Give branch managers one place to decide on refunds for their own branch.

## How the objectives are measured

| Objective | Measure | Target | Starting figure |
|-----------|---------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Each calendar month, the average time from Submitted to Paid (03) of the requests paid that month, overall and per branch | 3 days | Estimated at up to 10 days today; measured before launch (13 / OI-27) |
| 2 | Customer care's share of complaints that are about slow or lost refund requests | Set in 13 / OI-27 | 18% (Background) |
| 3 | Branches that decide every portal request in the portal and no longer record refunds on paper (the rollout plan, 13 / OI-15) | Every branch | 0 today |

The Head of Retail owns the objectives and reviews them every month with the Operations Lead, using the head-office refund report (09).
