# Definitions & Important Details

## Refund request lifecycle

A refund request is **Submitted** by the customer. The branch manager then **Approves** it (in full or in part) or **Rejects** it with a reason. An approved request becomes **Paid** once the payment provider accepts the payout; the money can take some days after that to appear on the customer's card. A customer can **Cancel** a request while it is still Submitted.

## Customer account and messages

A customer uses the portal with their own account, registered with a verified email address and, optionally, a mobile number. Wherever this BRD says the customer is told "by email and SMS", the SMS goes only when the account has a mobile number; the email always goes.

## Branch ownership

Each purchase, and so each refund request, belongs to exactly one branch. Branch managers decide only on their own branch's requests.
