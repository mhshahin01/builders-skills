# Reporting / Analytics

| Report | Audience | Frequency | Content |
|--------|----------|-----------|---------|
| Branch refund report | Branch Manager | Daily: one day per view, opened on demand ([UC-06](./06b-use-cases-branch-manager.md#uc-06-view-branch-refund-report)) | Requests per status, amounts paid, and average time to decision for the branch, counted as UC-06 step 3 states |
| Head-office refund report | Head of Retail and Operations Lead (head office; the role is 13 / OI-13) | Monthly | For each branch and in total: requests per status, average time from Submitted to Paid (01 Objective 1), average time to decision, approved and paid amounts, and payouts delayed (UC-04 E1) |
