# Reporting / Analytics

| Report / View | What It Shows | Audience | Frequency | Format |
|---------------|---------------|----------|-----------|--------|
| Monthly corrections | Every correction made in the month: the member, the points added or removed, the reason, who made it, and when. Each correction follows an upheld balance complaint (UC-03), and one complaint can need more than one correction. A month with no corrections is a month with no upheld complaints (NFR-01). | Loyalty Administrator | Monthly | Table, with export to CSV and Excel |
