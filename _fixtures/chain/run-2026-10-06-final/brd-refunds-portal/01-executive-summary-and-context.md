# Executive Summary

The Refunds Portal lets customers of our retail branches request and track refunds online. Branch managers approve or reject the requests in one place. Approved refunds are paid back to the customer's original card through our payment provider.

The main business problem it solves is slow and lost refund requests, handled today on paper and by phone. The portal replaces this with one recorded request that the customer can follow until it is paid.

Core capabilities:
- Request a refund online for a branch purchase.
- Track each refund request until it is paid.
- Cancel a request that is not decided yet.
- Approve a request in full or in part, or reject it with a reason.
- Pay approved refunds back to the original card, and tell the customer by email and SMS.

---

# Background and Context / Problem Statement

Customers of our retail branches ask for refunds in person today. Staff record the requests on paper, and managers approve them by phone. Customers wait 10 days on average and do not know where their request stands.

18% of complaints to customer care are about slow or lost refund requests. The monthly request volume is in [02 / Facts](./02-glossary-assumptions-facts.md#facts).

---

# Business Objectives

1. Cut the average time from request to payout from 10 days to 3 days. The time runs from Submitted to Paid. It includes the customer's trip to bring the items back to the branch.
2. Stop lost refund requests: every request is recorded and visible to the customer.
3. Give branch managers one place to decide on refunds for their own branch.
