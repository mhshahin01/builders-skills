# Reporting / Analytics

| Report / View | What It Shows | Audience | Frequency | Format |
|---------------|---------------|----------|-----------|--------|
| Monthly corrections | Every correction made in the month: the member, the points added or removed, the reason, who made it, and when. Each correction follows an upheld balance complaint (UC-03), and one complaint can need more than one correction. The report counts corrections. The LOYALTY owner counts distinct complaints upheld in each calendar month from the branches' complaint decisions, outside this product, for NFR-01; a correction date does not determine the complaint month. | Loyalty Administrator | Monthly | Table, with export to CSV and Excel |
