# 13. Services Decomposition (Summary)

All five rows are modules of the one deployable `refunds-platform` (ADR-01). Each module owns one schema of the `refunds_platform` database (ADR-06).

| Service | Type | Overview | Responsibility | Use cases (BRD) | Owns DB | Input | Output | Business Logic (Summary) | Integrations | Characteristics | Status |
|---------|------|----------|----------------|-----------------|---------|-------|--------|--------------------------|--------------|--------------------|--------|
| customer-accounts | module | Customer accounts of the Refunds Portal | Sign-up with a confirmed email address and mobile number, password reset, customer contact for messages, the linked-request count, account closure | [REFUNDS/UC-06](../brd-refunds-portal/06a-use-cases-customer.md#uc-06-sign-up-and-sign-in) | `customer_accounts` | REST from Refunds Portal web; events `RefundRequestSubmitted`, `RefundRequestUnlinked`; Schedules `account-closure`, `keycloak-user-reconciliation` | REST responses; Keycloak user changes; answers to port API-12 | Codes work 15 minutes and only the latest works; one account per email address; an account with no sign-in for 2 years and no linked request is closed | Keycloak admin client and token endpoint; notifications through API-14 | Low volume; holds PII (email address, mobile number); never stores passwords | Active |
| refund-requests | module | Refund request lifecycle | Receipt checks, submission, tracking, cancellation, branch-scoped decisions, branch assignments, the daily waiting-requests summary, the branch refund report, refund record retention | [REFUNDS/UC-01](../brd-refunds-portal/06a-use-cases-customer.md#uc-01-request-a-refund), [REFUNDS/UC-02](../brd-refunds-portal/06a-use-cases-customer.md#uc-02-track-refund-status), [REFUNDS/UC-03](../brd-refunds-portal/06a-use-cases-customer.md#uc-03-cancel-a-refund-request), [REFUNDS/UC-04](../brd-refunds-portal/06b-use-cases-branch-manager.md#uc-04-approve--reject-refund) | `refund_requests` | REST from customers and branch managers; events `PayoutSucceeded`, `PayoutFailedFinally`, and its own `RefundRequestSubmitted`, `RefundRequestCancelled`, `RefundRequestRejected`, `RefundPayoutFailed` (POS adapter); Schedules `waiting-requests-summary`, `branch-assignment-refresh`, `items-notice-send`, `refund-record-retention` | REST responses; the in-process events it publishes (§14.10); items notices (API-02); answers to port API-13 | Statuses Submitted, Approved, Rejected, Cancelled, Paid, Payout failed; a 30-day window counted in the branch's time zone; each item refunded once; refund capped at the card-paid amount; receipt discount split by price | POS Records (API-01, API-02); staff sign-in branch assignments (API-06) | Stateful; optimistic locking settles a cancellation racing a decision | Active |
| payouts | module | Payouts to the original card | Send each approved refund once, retry until the payout deadline (§17.3), report success or final failure | None - serves the payout steps of [REFUNDS/UC-04](../brd-refunds-portal/06b-use-cases-branch-manager.md#uc-04-approve--reject-refund) (steps 6-7, E1) | `payouts` | Event `RefundRequestApproved`; payout results (API-04); Schedule `payout-retry` | Payout calls (API-03); events `PayoutSucceeded`, `PayoutFailedFinally` | One payout per refund request; one key per attempt; the payout deadline from the first try (§17.3) | Payment Provider (API-03, API-04) | Moves money; carries REFUNDS/NFR-01 | Active |
| notifications | module | Email and SMS delivery | Turn refund events and code requests into messages to customers and branch managers | None - serves the customer and branch manager messages of [REFUNDS/UC-01](../brd-refunds-portal/06a-use-cases-customer.md#uc-01-request-a-refund), [REFUNDS/UC-03](../brd-refunds-portal/06a-use-cases-customer.md#uc-03-cancel-a-refund-request), [REFUNDS/UC-04](../brd-refunds-portal/06b-use-cases-branch-manager.md#uc-04-approve--reject-refund), and [REFUNDS/UC-06](../brd-refunds-portal/06a-use-cases-customer.md#uc-06-sign-up-and-sign-in) | `notifications` | Events `RefundRequestSubmitted`, `RefundRequestCancelled`, `RefundRequestRejected`, `RefundPaid`, `RefundPayoutFailed`, `WaitingRequestsSummarised`; port API-14; Schedule `message-send` | Message calls (API-05) | One template per message type and channel; recipients through API-12 and API-13; retries with backoff | Notification Partner (API-05) | Keeps a message log without message bodies | Active |
| loyalty-points | module | Points ledger | Earn points on purchases, take points back on paid refunds, opening balances, membership changes, corrections, the monthly corrections report, former-member retention | [LOYALTY/UC-01](../brd-loyalty-points/06a-use-cases-member.md#uc-01-view-points-balance), [LOYALTY/UC-02](../brd-loyalty-points/06a-use-cases-member.md#uc-02-view-points-history), [LOYALTY/UC-03](../brd-loyalty-points/06b-use-cases-loyalty-administrator.md#uc-03-correct-a-members-points) | `loyalty_points` | REST from members and Loyalty Administrators; member purchases (API-07), membership notices (API-08), opening balances (API-09); event `RefundPaid`; Schedules `former-member-retention`, `waiting-refund-check`, `balance-invariant-check` | REST responses with the balance, history, and report | 1 point per 1 EUR paid, rounded down; a take-back keeps the points of the amount not refunded and never takes the balance below 0; rejoin and go-live date rules | POS Records (API-07); member sign-in and membership (API-08); points balances at go-live (API-09) | Read-heavy (LOYALTY/NFR-05); balance always equals its movements (LOYALTY/NFR-01) | Active |
