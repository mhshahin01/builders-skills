# Executive Summary

The Refunds Portal lets customers of our retail branches request and track refunds online. Branch managers approve or reject the requests in one place. Approved refunds are paid back to the customer's original card through our payment provider.

The portal replaces paper records and phone approvals, so no refund request is lost and customers can see where each request stands.

Core capabilities:

- Customers request a refund for items on a branch receipt, follow it, and cancel it while it waits for a decision.
- Branch managers approve a request in full or in part, or reject it with a reason.
- Approved refunds are paid to the customer's original card.
- Customers are told about their request as [03 / Customer messages](./03-definitions-and-domain-concepts.md#customer-messages) sets out.

# Background and Context / Problem Statement

Customers ask for refunds in person at a branch today. Staff record the requests on paper. Managers approve them by phone. Customers wait up to 10 days without knowing where their request stands.
