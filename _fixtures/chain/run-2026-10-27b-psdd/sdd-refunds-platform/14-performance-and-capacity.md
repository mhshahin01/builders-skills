## 18.3 Peak Scenarios

| Scenario | Trigger | Expected Multiplier on Baseline | Mitigation |
|----------|---------|---------------------------------|------------|
| Seasonal sales | The business's seasonal sales ([REFUNDS 02 § Facts](../brd-refunds-portal/02-glossary-assumptions-facts.md#facts) Fact 2) | 3 × refund requests ([PSDD/CAP-04.1](../pre-sdd-refunds-platform/03b-register-capacity.md#cap-04); [REFUNDS/NFR-03](../brd-refunds-portal/10-nfrs.md#non-functional-requirements)) for about 3 weeks; points movements per [PSDD/QD-06](../pre-sdd-refunds-platform/10-validation-risks-unknowns.md#101-validation-and-unknowns) (open) | Horizontal scale-out of the deployable (§11.3) before the season; the payout and message outboxes absorb bursts without slowing user actions |
| Payout outage at CardPay Ltd | CardPay Ltd unavailable | Retries accumulate in the payout outbox | Circuit breaker and backoff (§12 INT-01); the branch manager is told after one day; no user action waits on it |
| Purchase feed backlog | Point-of-Sale Records delivers late or in a batch | Purchases arrive at once | Purchases keep their date ([LOYALTY 03 § Earning points](../brd-loyalty-points/03-definitions-and-domain-concepts.md#earning-points)); pending take-backs settle when they arrive |

## 18.4 Stress Testing Strategy

- **Tooling:** a load-testing tool run from the pipeline; the tool is a new dependency (AGENTS.md): **[NEEDS CLARIFICATION: Which load-testing tool does the product owner approve? Owner: product owner.]**
- **Environments:** stage, sized as prod ([§19](./15-environments.md#19-environments)).
- **Scenarios:** baseline at [PSDD/CAP-02.1](../pre-sdd-refunds-platform/03b-register-capacity.md#cap-02); the seasonal peak at 3 × ([PSDD/CAP-04.1](../pre-sdd-refunds-platform/03b-register-capacity.md#cap-04)) for one hour; 300 simultaneously active users ([PSDD/CAP-03.1](../pre-sdd-refunds-platform/03b-register-capacity.md#cap-03)); a zone failover drill against the AZ RTO and RPO of [§8.6.4](./05a-infrastructure-and-network.md#864-backup-and-disaster-recovery); a region failover drill against the region rows of §8.6.4; a CardPay Ltd outage with a stub.
- **Acceptance criteria:** everyday actions within 2,000 ms at the edge ([PSDD/CAP-07.1](../pre-sdd-refunds-platform/03b-register-capacity.md#cap-07), [PSDD/CAP-07.2](../pre-sdd-refunds-platform/03b-register-capacity.md#cap-07)) at the seasonal peak; no payout sent twice after a failover; take-backs within 1 hour ([LOYALTY/NFR-02](../brd-loyalty-points/10-nfrs.md#non-functional-requirements)).
- **Cadence:** before go-live, before each seasonal sale, and after a change to a module's persistence.
- **Reporting:** results stored with the release in the repository.
