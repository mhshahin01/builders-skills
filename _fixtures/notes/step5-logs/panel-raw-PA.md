Concern: Refund-to-payout saga has no terminal failure outcome, and its deadline runs on two clocks in two deployables
Where: SDD 13b §17.2 Business Logic (After the retry window), Constraints, Figures 18 and 20; SDD 13a §17.1 Business Logic (Payout watchdog), Retention Policy; SDD 19 §24.8.1

The platform has "no manual retry, other payout route, or closing of a refund", and §24.8.1 rolls nothing back. So a permanent refusal (a closed or replaced card) never ends.
