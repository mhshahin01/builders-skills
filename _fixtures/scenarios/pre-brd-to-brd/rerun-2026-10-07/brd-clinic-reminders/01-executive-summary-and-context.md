# Executive Summary

Clinic Reminders helps small private clinics in Cairo and Giza reduce empty appointment slots and reminder calls. Receptionists enter appointments, patients answer reminders, and clinic owners receive a weekly report. The product covers clinics with 1 to 5 doctors in dentistry, dermatology and pediatrics. Source: [pre-BRD 01](../../run/pre-brd-clinic-reminders/01-concept-sheet.md) and [pre-BRD 02](../../run/pre-brd-clinic-reminders/02-product-charter.md).

The requirements cover the Must and Should features in [04](./04-scope-and-personas.md). They describe the requested product; they do not approve the investment or fill the pre-BRD's unanswered questions.

# Background and Context / Problem Statement

Receptionists use calls, paper and personal messages to chase appointments. Patients forget visits or cancel too late to fill the slot. The market has reminder tools and substitutes, while demand, cost and local results remain unproven. Research, figures and competitive evidence stay in [pre-BRD 06](../../run/pre-brd-clinic-reminders/06-market-comparison.md), [pre-BRD 07](../../run/pre-brd-clinic-reminders/07-market-sizing-analysis.md), [pre-BRD 08](../../run/pre-brd-clinic-reminders/08-pestle-analysis.md), [pre-BRD 09](../../run/pre-brd-clinic-reminders/09-porters-five-forces.md), [pre-BRD 10](../../run/pre-brd-clinic-reminders/10-efas.md), [pre-BRD 11](../../run/pre-brd-clinic-reminders/11-ifas.md) and [pre-BRD 12](../../run/pre-brd-clinic-reminders/12-swot.md).

The discovery verdict is **No-Go**, cited in [pre-BRD 22 Scoreboard](../../run/pre-brd-clinic-reminders/22-executive-summary-scoreboard.md#result) and [pre-BRD 23 Investor Assessment](../../run/pre-brd-clinic-reminders/23-investor-assessment.md#verdict). Conditions concern market reach, defensibility, team and funding, local validation, and counsel-confirmed legal treatment. Their evidence stays at those links. **[NEEDS CLARIFICATION: Founders to decide whether and when to commit to the build after the No-Go; pre-BRD 24 OI-01 remains Open.]**
