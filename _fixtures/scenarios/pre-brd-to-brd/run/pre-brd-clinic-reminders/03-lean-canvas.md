# Lean Canvas

A one-page business plan template designed for startups and innovators. It helps map out the most critical elements of a business idea quickly and clearly. Adapted from the Business Model Canvas and optimized for early-stage products where uncertainty is high. It emphasizes real-world problems and customer segments, and focuses on problems rather than features so the team avoids building things nobody needs.

| Section | What to capture | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Problem | Top 1 to 3 problems the product solves. | (1) No-shows and late cancellations waste paid doctor time; (2) reminder calls consume receptionist time and often go unanswered; (3) freed slots stay empty because waiting patients cannot be reached fast. Evidence in [01-concept-sheet.md](01-concept-sheet.md). Existing alternatives: manual calls, personal or WhatsApp Business app messages, booking-platform confirmations, and overbooking ([09-porters-five-forces.md](09-porters-five-forces.md), Substitutes). |
| Customer Segments | Target users or personas. | See [01-concept-sheet.md](01-concept-sheet.md), Target Audience. Early adopters: owner-doctors of 1 to 2 doctor dental and dermatology clinics in Cairo and Giza who already message patients from a personal WhatsApp. |
| Unique Value Proposition | Why the solution stands out. | See [01-concept-sheet.md](01-concept-sheet.md), UVP. High-level concept: "a receptionist that never forgets to remind and refills cancelled slots." |
| Solution | Main features or services. | Problem 1: reminders with one-reply confirm or cancel. Problem 2: automatic WhatsApp-first sending with SMS fallback. Problem 3: waitlist refill plus the weekly no-show report. Full feature list in 01. |
| Channels | How users are reached. Owned (website, blog, email), paid (ads, social, influencers), partner (resellers, affiliates), sales (inside sales, field sales, product-led growth). | Sales: founder-led field visits to clinics in Cairo and Giza, then one sales hire. Partner: dental supply distributors and specialty society events. Owned: Arabic landing page with a no-show cost calculator and WhatsApp click-to-chat; the weekly report itself. Paid: Facebook and Instagram ads targeted at doctors. Referral: one free month per referred paying clinic. Roles, phases, and owners in [21-roadmap-project-plan.md](21-roadmap-project-plan.md). |
| Revenue Streams | How the product makes money. | (1) Monthly subscription per clinic in EGP, tiered by number of doctors and including a monthly reminder allowance; (2) message top-ups above the allowance (WhatsApp and SMS pass-through plus margin); (3) annual prepayment at a two-month discount. Planning ARPU EGP 650 a month, EGP 7,800 a year ([07-market-sizing-analysis.md](07-market-sizing-analysis.md)); price points in [21](21-roadmap-project-plan.md). |
| Cost Structure | Key costs to operate. | Year-one cost test (EGP, 2026-10-01 to 2027-09-30, canonical rate EGP 52.06 per USD; salary and cost sources in [11-ifas.md](11-ifas.md)): three developers at employer cost EGP 832,725 at the Cairo median of EGP 20,000 a month (each EGP 20,000 + 3,131.25 employer social insurance = 23,131.25 x 12 = 277,575), EGP 1,414,845 at five-year pay (EGP 36,170), or EGP 1,736,757 at top-decile pay (EGP 45,112); one field sales hire EGP 325,527 for 12 months (upper bound; the roadmap hires in Q2-2027, about EGP 162,764 in year one); hosting about EGP 49,009 (USD 941.40). Residual for messaging, DPO and legal, incorporation, marketing, and contingency: EGP 792,739, EGP 210,619, or an overrun of EGP 111,293. Variable messaging: each 1,000 reminded appointments cost EGP 187 (reminder only) to EGP 375 (reminder plus confirmation reply) on WhatsApp before 14% VAT, the reply being free within 1,000 service messages a month; SMS fallback costs EGP 0.14 to 1.00 a segment, two segments for a long Arabic message ([09](09-porters-five-forces.md), Suppliers). [NEEDS CLARIFICATION: What are the average monthly appointments per target clinic and the share of patients who will need SMS fallback? Both drive messaging cost.] Fixed compliance: PDPC licence fees (small at low volume), DPO, counsel. |
| Key Metrics | How success is measured. | See [01-concept-sheet.md](01-concept-sheet.md), Success Metrics. Pilot metric that matters: relative no-show reduction per clinic against its own baseline. |
| Unfair Advantage | Hard-to-copy benefits. | None proven yet. Candidates: the only automatic waitlist refill and SMS fallback among the five compared products ([06-market-comparison.md](06-market-comparison.md)), a growing cross-clinic no-show dataset, and early clinic relationships in Cairo and Giza; tested in [18-vrio.md](18-vrio.md). |
| Key Partners | Entities or individuals who help deliver the value proposition. | Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform, direct Cloud API); an NTRA-licensed SMS aggregator; a cloud host (in Egypt or abroad per the PDPL decision); Egyptian data-protection counsel and an outsourced or in-house DPO; a local payment gateway; dental supply distributors and specialty societies as channel partners. |
| Key Activities | The most important tasks to deliver value, attract users, and maintain operations. | Build and operate the messaging pipeline; onboard clinics (import, templates, consent); founder-led field sales; PDPL licensing, consent records, and breach readiness; Arabic support on WhatsApp. |
| Key Resources | The assets (human, digital, financial) needed to operate the business. | Three founding developers; a verified Meta business portfolio with approved utility templates; a registered SMS sender ID; the PDPC licence and a DPO; pilot clinic relationships; the EGP 2,000,000 first-year budget. |
| Customer Relationships | How customers are acquired, retained, and grown. | Assisted, in-person onboarding by a founder; an Arabic WhatsApp support line; the weekly no-show report as the retention habit; referral credit for clinics that bring other clinics. |
