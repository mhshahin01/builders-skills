# Value Proposition Canvas

A strategic tool used to ensure the product or service is aligned with customer needs. It has two sides: the customer profile (jobs, pains, gains) and the value map (products and services, pain relievers, gain creators). Product-market fit is achieved when the value map matches the customer profile.

Fill one table per customer persona. Duplicate the block below for each persona and set the persona name.

## Persona: _Clinic owner (doctor-owner, the buyer)_

| Section | What to capture | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Customer Jobs | What the customer is trying to get done (functional, emotional, or social). | Functional: keep every paid hour of the schedule filled; know how many appointments were lost and why. Emotional: stop worrying about empty chairs. Social: run a clinic that feels organised and modern to patients. |
| Pains | Challenges, obstacles, or risks the customer faces. | No-shows and late cancellations leave paid doctor time idle (scale of the problem in [10-efas.md](10-efas.md)); no reliable no-show number to act on; paying staff to make reminder calls; fear of liability when patient data sits in personal phones. |
| Gains | Outcomes or benefits the customer desires. | Fuller, predictable days; one weekly number that shows whether the clinic is improving; no extra hire; a service priced in EGP that pays for itself. |
| Products & Services | What the product offers. | The Clinic Reminders subscription (feature list in [01-concept-sheet.md](01-concept-sheet.md), Key Features). |
| Pain Relievers | How the product reduces or eliminates customer pains. | Automatic reminders with one-reply confirm or cancel turn silent no-shows into early cancellations; freed slots go straight to the waitlist; consent and opt-out are recorded per patient so data handling follows the PDPL path in [08-pestle-analysis.md](08-pestle-analysis.md). |
| Gain Creators | How the product creates benefits for the customer. | The weekly no-show report shows the no-show rate, confirmed share, and slots refilled from the waitlist, so the owner sees the return on the subscription in clinic terms. |

## Persona: _Receptionist (the daily user)_

| Section | What to capture | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Customer Jobs | What the customer is trying to get done (functional, emotional, or social). | Functional: confirm tomorrow's list, handle cancellations, rebook from the waiting list, answer patient calls. Emotional: finish the day without chasing patients. Social: be seen by the doctor as keeping the schedule under control. |
| Pains | Challenges, obstacles, or risks the customer faces. | Hours of reminder calls that go unanswered; patients who cancel by not showing up; a waiting list kept on paper or in a personal phone; blame for empty slots. |
| Gains | Outcomes or benefits the customer desires. | A morning view of who confirmed, who cancelled, and who has not replied; fewer calls; rebooking without a phone marathon. |
| Products & Services | What the product offers. | Web dashboard with the day's appointment statuses, appointment entry and CSV import, and the clinic waitlist. |
| Pain Relievers | How the product reduces or eliminates customer pains. | Reminders send themselves; patient replies update the appointment status automatically; a cancelled slot is offered to waitlisted patients in order without calls. |
| Gain Creators | How the product creates benefits for the customer. | Calls are needed only for the short "no reply" list, so the receptionist spends time on patients at the desk. |

## Persona: _Patient (the message recipient)_

| Section | What to capture | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Customer Jobs | What the customer is trying to get done (functional, emotional, or social). | Functional: remember the appointment; cancel or confirm without a call; get seen sooner when a slot opens. Emotional: avoid the awkwardness of cancelling. Social: be a reliable patient. |
| Pains | Challenges, obstacles, or risks the customer faces. | Forgetting the appointment; a busy clinic line when trying to cancel; long waits for the next free slot; unwanted messages after the visit. |
| Gains | Outcomes or benefits the customer desires. | A reminder on the app already used every day, in Arabic; one-tap answers; an earlier slot when someone else cancels. |
| Products & Services | What the product offers. | WhatsApp reminder with confirm and cancel buttons; an SMS with a confirm or cancel link when WhatsApp is not delivered; waitlist slot offers. |
| Pain Relievers | How the product reduces or eliminates customer pains. | The reminder arrives before the visit, and cancelling takes one reply or one tap instead of a call; an opt-out reply stops all messages. |
| Gain Creators | How the product creates benefits for the customer. | Waitlisted patients receive earlier slots that would otherwise stay empty. |
