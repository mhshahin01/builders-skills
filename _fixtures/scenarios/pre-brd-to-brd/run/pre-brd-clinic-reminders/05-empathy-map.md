# Empathy Map

A collaborative visualization tool used to understand users better. It captures what a user says, thinks, does, and feels, plus their pains and gains, putting the team in the user's shoes and aligning product design with real needs and desires.

Fill one table per persona. Duplicate the block below for each persona and set the persona name.

## Persona: _Clinic owner (doctor-owner, the buyer)_

| Section | What to capture | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Says | What the user says out loud in interviews or feedback. | Hypothesis, to validate in pilot interviews: "Half my evening list did not come and nobody called." "My receptionist already sends WhatsApp messages, why pay?" |
| Thinks | What the user thinks but might not voice directly. | "If I knew my real no-show number I could act on it." "Another subscription in EGP when costs keep rising." |
| Does | What the user actually does and how they behave. | Overbooks to cover no-shows; asks staff to call patients the day before; checks the day's list on a phone between patients. |
| Feels | The user's emotional state, concerns, and desires. | Frustrated by idle paid hours; wary of tools that add work; protective of patient trust. |
| Pains | Frustrations or challenges. | Functional pains in [04-value-proposition-canvas.md](04-value-proposition-canvas.md); emotional: feeling that lost time is invisible and out of control. |
| Gains | What the user hopes to achieve. | Functional gains in 04; emotional: confidence that the schedule will hold. |

## Persona: _Receptionist (the daily user)_

| Section | What to capture | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Says | What the user says out loud in interviews or feedback. | Hypothesis, to validate in pilot interviews: "I call everyone the day before and half do not answer." "Patients say they never got the message." |
| Thinks | What the user thinks but might not voice directly. | "The doctor blames me for empty slots." "A new system means more typing." |
| Does | What the user actually does and how they behave. | Calls and sends personal WhatsApp messages from the clinic phone; keeps the waiting list on paper; re-enters appointments in a notebook or simple software. |
| Feels | The user's emotional state, concerns, and desires. | Overloaded at peak hours; anxious about the doctor's reaction to gaps; relieved by anything that removes calls. |
| Pains | Frustrations or challenges. | Functional pains in 04; emotional: being the one held responsible for patients' behaviour. |
| Gains | What the user hopes to achieve. | Functional gains in 04; emotional: a calm morning with a list that is already confirmed. |

## Persona: _Patient (the message recipient)_

| Section | What to capture | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Says | What the user says out loud in interviews or feedback. | Hypothesis, to validate in pilot interviews: "I forgot, sorry." "I tried to call to cancel but nobody picked up." |
| Thinks | What the user thinks but might not voice directly. | "Cancelling by phone is awkward." "Who else gets my number and my appointment details?" |
| Does | What the user actually does and how they behave. | Books by phone or WhatsApp; relies on memory or a phone alarm; skips the visit when plans change instead of calling. |
| Feels | The user's emotional state, concerns, and desires. | Busy and distracted; mildly guilty about missing visits; sensitive to spam. |
| Pains | Frustrations or challenges. | Functional pains in 04; emotional: embarrassment about cancelling or missing a visit. |
| Gains | What the user hopes to achieve. | Functional gains in 04; emotional: feeling respected by a clinic that makes changes easy. |
