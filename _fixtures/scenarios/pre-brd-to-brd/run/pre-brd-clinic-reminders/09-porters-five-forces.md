# Porter's Five Forces

A tool to analyze the competitive pressure of the market on a similar product or feature. It measures the level of risk or threat for the product.

## Scoring rule

Each force is rated on a threat scale from 1 to 3: 1 = low risk / threat, 2 = medium, 3 = high risk / threat. The total score is the average of the five force ratings.

| # | Force | Your review | Threat level (1=Low, 2=Med, 3=High) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Competitive Rivalry | High. Vezeeta had 27,762 network doctors and 9,446 private clinics across its six markets in 2024 and served about 1.05M patients in Egypt ([Team Fund Health, 2025](https://teamfundhealth.org/uploads/final-2025-09-17-2024-vezeeta-impact-spotlight.pdf)); its doctor app already sends confirmation messages to cut no-shows ([Google Play, 2026](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.drbridge.Doctors&hl=en_US)). Clinic software with WhatsApp reminders is crowded: the five products in [06-market-comparison.md](06-market-comparison.md) plus Tabbaba at EGP 799 to 3,299 a month ([Tabbaba, 2026](https://tabbaba.com/en/)), ClinicGateway at EGP 2,500 a month ([ClinicGateway, 2026](https://clinicgateway.ae/blog/best-clinic-management-software-egypt-2025/)), SofClinic, SYNAX ([SYNAX](https://www.synax.cloud/en)), and MD System, whose WhatsApp integration is free ([MD System](https://md-system.online/)). WhatsApp bot tools let anyone assemble reminders: ArabyBot from EGP 1,000 a month plus Meta fees ([ArabyBot](https://arabybot.com/)), Wati from USD 99 a month ([Wati](https://www.wati.io/pricing/)). Basic reminders are near-commodity; automatic waitlist refill and SMS fallback are offered by none of the five products compared in 06. | 3 |
| 2 | Threat of New Entrants | High. Entry needs little capital and has no network effects: a tech provider can onboard 10 businesses per rolling 7 days, 200 after verification ([Meta, Embedded Signup](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/embedded-signup/)), and a new portfolio starts at 250 unique users a day ([Meta, Messaging limits](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/messaging-limits/)); small entrants keep appearing (SofClinic claims 5 clinics, [SofClinic](https://sofclinic.com/en/)). Entry is slowed, not blocked, by PDPL licensing ([08-pestle-analysis.md](08-pestle-analysis.md), Legal), sender-ID registration of about 15 business days ([D7 Networks](https://d7networks.com/blog/send-sms-in-egypt/)), Infobip's rule that only direct-contract entities register local sender IDs ([Infobip](https://www.infobip.com/docs/essentials/mena-registration/egypt-letter-of-authorization-loa-guidelines)), and thinner funding: Egyptian startups raised USD 158.9M across 29 deals in H1 2026, down 11% ([Wamda, 2026](https://www.wamda.com/2026/07/mena-startups-raise-1-7-billion-h1-2026-despite-regional-uncertainty)). | 3 |
| 3 | Bargaining Power of Buyers | High. The 89,146 licensed clinics (canonical figures in [07-market-sizing-analysis.md](07-market-sizing-analysis.md)) are small and cost-pressured (urban inflation 14.5%, Aug 2026); price anchors run from Vezeeta's EGP 599.99 profile activation fee ([App Store Egypt](https://apps.apple.com/eg/app/vezeeta-for-doctors/id964542075)) to EGP 799 a month for all-inclusive WhatsApp reminder plans ([Tabbaba](https://tabbaba.com/en/)); switching costs are low (14-day trials with no card at Tabbaba; 7-day trial at Wati); and the free WhatsApp Business app is a do-it-yourself option ([WhatsApp Business](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/business-app/)). Fragmentation is the only factor that weakens buyer leverage. | 3 |
| 4 | Bargaining Power of Suppliers | High. Meta is the single source of the primary channel: it sets Egypt prices in USD (utility USD 0.0036 = EGP 0.19), template categories, and limits, and can reprice each quarter ([Meta, Pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing)); from 2026-10-01 it charges service messages beyond a free monthly allowance ([360dialog, 2026](https://360dialog.com/blog/whatsapp-service-message-charging-october-2026/)) and in-window utility replies ([Meta, Non-template messages](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/non-template-messages)), detail in [10-efas.md](10-efas.md), T1. SMS is more competitive: EGP 0.14 to 0.24 per SMS in WE bundles ([WE Business SMS](https://te.eg/web/te-business/we-business-sms)), EGP 0.35 to 1.00 at SMS Misr ([SMS Misr](https://www.smsmisr.com/)), against EGP 20.61 (USD 0.3959) through Twilio ([Twilio](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/sms/pricing/eg)), but each of the four carriers requires sender-ID registration. Cloud is commoditized; Huawei Cloud Cairo enables in-country hosting ([Huawei, 2024](https://www.huawei.com/en/news/2024/5/huawei-cloud-goes-live-in-egypt)). | 3 |
| 5 | Threat of Substitutes | High. Receptionists already call or message patients from the free WhatsApp Business app or a personal phone ([WhatsApp Business](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/business-app/)); broadcast lists cap at 256 contacts who saved the clinic's number ([WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/861663048350950/)); booking platforms bundle their own no-show tools, such as Vezeeta's confirmations and V-Pay prepayment ([Google Play, 2026](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.drbridge.Doctors&hl=en_US)); overbooking or doing nothing costs no cash. Where manual substitutes break down depends on appointment volume per clinic, which is open in [03-lean-canvas.md](03-lean-canvas.md), Cost Structure. | 3 |
