# Product Strategy Canvas

A structured visual tool that helps product teams define and align the strategic direction of a product. It captures the why, who, what, and how of the product to ensure clarity, focus, and alignment across product, design, tech, and business teams, and to support prioritization and pitching.

| Element | What to capture | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Vision | The long-term purpose and inspiration behind the product. Why are we building it? | See [02-product-charter.md](02-product-charter.md), Purpose / Vision. Strategic framing: win Greater Cairo's small clinics on no-show recovery first, then extend the messaging layer to the same clinics, and only then enter new geographies ([17-ansoff-matrix.md](17-ansoff-matrix.md)). |
| Target Market | The specific customer segments or personas you aim to serve. | See [01-concept-sheet.md](01-concept-sheet.md), Target Audience; the beachhead is in [21-roadmap-project-plan.md](21-roadmap-project-plan.md), Go-To-Market. |
| Customer Needs | The key pain points, desires, or jobs-to-be-done for those segments. | See [04-value-proposition-canvas.md](04-value-proposition-canvas.md) and [05-empathy-map.md](05-empathy-map.md). Strategic order: (1) kept or refilled slots; (2) no reminder calls; (3) a weekly number the owner trusts. |
| Value Proposition | What makes the product uniquely valuable to those customers. | See [01-concept-sheet.md](01-concept-sheet.md), UVP. |
| Differentiators | How the product stands out from competitors. | Against the booking marketplace (Vezeeta): WhatsApp-first with two-way replies, and the patient relationship stays with the clinic. Against clinic software (TabeebPlus, iClinicOS, PDental, Dentolize): a focused add-on for clinics that run on paper, spreadsheets, or a booking app, with the only automatic waitlist refill and SMS fallback among the five and an owner digest pushed weekly ([06-market-comparison.md](06-market-comparison.md)). These are temporary advantages ([18-vrio.md](18-vrio.md)); the defensible layer to build is the cross-clinic no-show dataset. |
| Success Metrics | How success will be measured (retention, revenue, adoption). | See [01-concept-sheet.md](01-concept-sheet.md), Success Metrics; targets in [15-okrs.md](15-okrs.md). |
| Channels | Where and how customers are reached (sales, marketing, support). | See [03-lean-canvas.md](03-lean-canvas.md), Channels, and [21-roadmap-project-plan.md](21-roadmap-project-plan.md), Channels & launch plan. |
| Revenue Model | How the product will make money. | See [03-lean-canvas.md](03-lean-canvas.md), Revenue Streams. |
| Cost Structure | The main cost drivers (tech, support, marketing, operations). | See [03-lean-canvas.md](03-lean-canvas.md), Cost Structure. |
