# Executive Summary

Customers earn points on every purchase in our branches. Today they cannot see their balance or use their points without calling customer care. Loyalty Points lets customers see their balance and turn points into vouchers online, and lets loyalty managers correct a customer's points. When a purchase is refunded, the points earned on it are taken back.

# Background and Context

About 200,000 customers hold points. Customer care receives about 3,000 balance questions a month.

# Business Objectives

1. Customers see their points balance at any time without calling customer care.
2. Customers turn points into vouchers online in under a minute.
3. Points always match what the customer really bought, including refunds.
