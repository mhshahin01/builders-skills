# Definitions & Important Details

## Points balance

A customer's balance is the points earned on purchases, minus the points spent on vouchers, plus or minus adjustments. When a purchase is refunded, in full or in part, the points earned on the refunded amount are taken back. The balance never goes below zero: if the points were already spent, the balance stays at zero and the difference is recorded for the loyalty manager.
