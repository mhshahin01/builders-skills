# 12. Integrations

Partner, purpose, information exchanged, direction, and criticality come from [BRD Integrations](../brd-refunds-portal/08-integrations.md#integrations); this table adds the technical fields. Provider-owned fields (protocol, authentication, provider rate limits) live in the API contracts of §15, which stay `TBD - external` until the provider documentation is supplied; the columns below reference them instead of repeating them.

| Integration ID | What (System) | Purpose | How (Protocol / Mode) | When (Trigger) | Auth | Timeout | Rate Limit | Retries & Backoff | Fallback | Notes |
|----------------|---------------|---------|------------------------|----------------|------|---------|------------|--------------------|-----------| ------|
| INT-01 | POS Records (Retail IT team) | Look up a receipt: its items, amounts, branch, and purchase date | Synchronous request-response while the customer waits; protocol per API-01 (TBD - external) | Receipt lookup and the re-check at submission ([UC-01](../brd-refunds-portal/06a-use-cases-customer.md#uc-01-request-a-refund) steps 2 and 5) | Per API-01 (TBD - external); credentials in the secrets manager (§6) | **[NEEDS CLARIFICATION: per-call timeout, within the receipt-check response-time target of §18]** | Provider limit per API-01 (TBD - external) | Retried on timeouts and 5xx with exponential backoff and jitter (the lookup is a read). **[NEEDS CLARIFICATION: attempt count]** | Circuit breaker and bulkhead on the adapter; when POS Records is unavailable the customer is told the receipt cannot be checked now and to try again later, and no request is created | Owner: `refund` (§17.1). Contract: API-01. Criticality: Critical. Risks R-04, R-05. |
| INT-02 | Payment Provider - CardPay Ltd | Pay approved refunds back to the original card; receive payout results | Outbound: synchronous call from the payout dispatcher, asynchronous to users (driven by persisted payout state); inbound: payout results per API-03 (mechanism TBD - external) | A payout becomes due after `REFUND_APPROVED` ([UC-04](../brd-refunds-portal/06b-use-cases-branch-manager.md#uc-04-approve--reject-refund) step 6) and at every retry; results when CardPay reports them (step 7) | Per API-02 and API-03 (TBD - external); credentials in the secrets manager (§6) | **[NEEDS CLARIFICATION: per-attempt timeout]** | Provider limit per API-02 (TBD - external) | Exponential backoff with jitter within the escalation window of §17.2, always with the payout's idempotency key; an unknown outcome is resolved before any new attempt. **[NEEDS CLARIFICATION: retry intervals and attempt cap]** | Circuit breaker and bulkhead on the adapter; the request stays Approved, and the branch manager is told when the escalation window passes ([UC-04](../brd-refunds-portal/06b-use-cases-branch-manager.md#uc-04-approve--reject-refund) E1) | Owner: `payout` (§17.2). Contracts: API-02, API-03. Criticality: Critical. Hard dependency ([BRD Dependencies](../brd-refunds-portal/02-glossary-assumptions-facts.md#dependencies) 1). Risks R-02, R-03, R-04. |
| INT-03 | Notification Partner - MsgHub | Send customer messages by email and SMS, and the branch-manager escalation message | Outbound synchronous call from the message dispatcher, asynchronous to users (driven by persisted message state); protocol per API-04 (TBD - external) | A message becomes due when `notification` handles `REFUND_REQUEST_SUBMITTED`, `REFUND_REQUEST_CANCELLED`, `REFUND_REJECTED`, `REFUND_PAID`, or `REFUND_PAYOUT_ESCALATED` (§14.5) | Per API-04 (TBD - external); credentials in the secrets manager (§6) | **[NEEDS CLARIFICATION: per-call timeout]** | Provider limit per API-04 (TBD - external) | Exponential backoff with jitter, always with the message's idempotency key. **[NEEDS CLARIFICATION: attempt count and give-up window]** | Circuit breaker and bulkhead on the adapter; the message stays pending and is retried; after the last attempt it moves to the dead-letter table with an alert. The refund flow never waits for a message. | Owner: `notification` (§17.3). Contract: API-04. Criticality: Important. |
