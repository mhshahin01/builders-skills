# Project Scope

Customers see their points and turn them into vouchers online. Loyalty managers correct points when something went wrong.

## In Scope

- Points balance and history for customers.
- Vouchers bought with points.
- Adjustments by loyalty managers.
- Points taken back when a purchase is refunded.

## Out of Scope

- Earning points on online-shop purchases.

---

# Personas / Actors

| Persona | Role | Key Goals | Access Level |
|---------|------|-----------|-------------|
| Customer | Buyer who holds a loyalty card | See their points and use them | Own points only |
| Loyalty Manager | Runs the loyalty programme | Keep every customer's points correct | All customers |
